Handwritten notes, on your computer and your tablet

Inkleaf is a note-taking app for writing by hand: notebooks with real paper styles, writing on PDFs, shapes and maths symbols that tidy themselves up, and a daftar annossous (دفتر النصوص) laid out like the printed books Moroccan teachers use.

  • Pen, highlighter, shapes, text, sticky notes and pictures, with pressure-sensitive pens.
  • Write on any PDF; export to PDF, PowerPoint or PNG.
  • A daftar with your classes, timetable, student lists and holidays filled in for you.
  • Windows, Linux and Android tablets. Your notebooks can stay the same on all of them through your own Google Drive.

Download

Installers for Windows, Linux (AppImage) and Android are on the releases page.

Your notes stay yours

Inkleaf keeps your notes on your device. If you turn on sync, they go to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive and nowhere else: there is no Inkleaf server. See the privacy policy.
